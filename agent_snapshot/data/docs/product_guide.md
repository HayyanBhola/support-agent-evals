# GlowCare Product Guide (FICTIONAL DEMO DOCUMENT)

## Hydra Balance Moisturizer
A lightweight, oil-free daily moisturizer built around hyaluronic acid and niacinamide. Designed for oily, combination, and normal skin types that want hydration without a heavy or greasy feel. Absorbs quickly, layers well under sunscreen or makeup.

## Barrier Repair Cream
A rich, ceramide-based cream for dry, sensitive, or compromised skin. Formulated to help restore the skin's natural moisture barrier using a blend of ceramides, cholesterol, fatty acids, and shea butter. Best used at night, can be layered over serums for extra nourishment.

## Niacinamide Serum
A 10% niacinamide serum targeting visible pores, uneven texture, and excess oil. Suited to oily and combination skin. New users are advised to introduce it gradually (every other day) to assess tolerance.

## Gentle Daily Cleanser
A sulfate-free, low-pH cleanser suitable for all skin types, including sensitive skin. Designed to remove impurities without stripping the skin's natural moisture.

## Lightweight Sunscreen SPF 50
A broad-spectrum mineral sunscreen (zinc oxide and titanium dioxide based) with a light, non-greasy finish. Suitable for daily wear across all skin types, including oily and sensitive skin.

## General Routine Guidance
A simple starting routine: Cleanser → (optional Serum) → Moisturizer → Sunscreen (AM), and Cleanser → (optional Serum) → Moisturizer (PM). This is general guidance only, not a personalized medical or dermatological recommendation.
