# GlowCare Ingredients Guide (FICTIONAL DEMO DOCUMENT)

This guide explains, in plain language, the role of key ingredients used across GlowCare products. This is general cosmetic-ingredient information, not medical advice.

**Hyaluronic Acid** — A humectant that helps draw and hold moisture in the skin, contributing to a plumper, hydrated appearance.

**Niacinamide** — A form of vitamin B3 commonly used to help with the appearance of pores, uneven texture, and oil balance.

**Ceramides** — Lipids naturally found in skin that help support the skin's barrier function, reducing moisture loss.

**Zinc PCA** — Often used alongside niacinamide to help regulate excess oil production.

**Glycerin** — A widely used humectant that helps attract water to the skin's surface.

**Dimethicone** — A silicone-based ingredient that gives products a smooth, lightweight feel and can help reduce moisture loss.

**Zinc Oxide / Titanium Dioxide** — Mineral UV filters used in sunscreens to help protect skin from UVA/UVB rays.

**Panthenol** — A form of provitamin B5 used to help soothe and moisturize skin.

**Allantoin** — An ingredient known for its soothing, skin-conditioning properties.

## Important Note
This guide describes general ingredient functions and does not make claims that any GlowCare product treats, cures, or prevents any medical or dermatological condition. Customers with known allergies or sensitivities should review full ingredient lists and consult a dermatologist if uncertain.
